# Erasmus office FAQ (sample content for the demo)

**When is the deadline for the insurance document?**
Send your insurance document before 15 January for the spring semester, or before 15 August for the autumn semester.

**When is the deadline for the Learning Agreement?**
Send your signed Learning Agreement before 15 December for the spring semester, or before 1 July for the autumn semester.

**Who signs my Learning Agreement?**
You, your faculty's Erasmus coordinator and the receiving university all sign it.

**What documents do I need to send?**
A Learning Agreement, a Transcript of Records, an insurance document and, after you arrive, a Certificate of Arrival.

**When do I send the Certificate of Arrival?**
Within 7 days of arriving at your host university.

**Did you get my document?**
Staff can confirm this once the document has been filed. You do not need to send it again.

**What are the office hours?**
Monday to Thursday, 10:00 to 15:00.
